/008Questions

Honest answers, before you ask.

Is my data safe?

Your apps connect through scoped grants you approve yourself and can revoke anytime. I never hold your passwords. Your agents run on isolated infrastructure I manage, and the access to your tools stays yours to grant and yours to pull.

What does unlimited actually mean?

As many agents and as many workflows as your business can use, inside one flat number. In practice most teams find that two or three well-built agents carry a huge load, but you'll never have to ration an idea or watch a meter. If a workflow would help, we build it.

What happens when something breaks?

Usually you never find out. Every agent runs with a watchdog that restores it automatically, and my agents alert me the moment a job fails, so I'm fixing things before you notice them. You become dependent on these fast, which is exactly why reliability is my job and my only job.

What can't it do?

It won't replace your judgment. Anything consequential ships as a draft and waits for your approval. And while it collaborates in shared channels with your team and their agents, it has one owner, and that's you.

How fast is setup, really?

48 hours from our call to live, counting from when you finish the intake and click your connect links. Your part takes about fifteen minutes. The rest of those two days is me building.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, no minimum. When the retainer ends I decommission the agents. Everything that matters lives in accounts you own, so there's nothing of yours for me to keep.

Why Slack?

Because you already live there, so there's no new app to remember to open. Your Wingman shows up as a teammate in the sidebar; you can DM it like a person or give it its own channel. Not on Slack? A free workspace takes five minutes and I'll set it up with you during onboarding.

Do I need to be technical?

No. That is the entire point of this offer. If you can send a Slack message and click an approve button, you can run a Wingman. The technical parts are my job, permanently.
