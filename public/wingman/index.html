/007Questions

Honest answers, before you ask.

Is my data safe?

Your Wingman runs on your accounts. Your apps connect through scoped grants you approve yourself and can revoke anytime, and your AI account links the same way. I never hold your passwords or your API keys. I manage the machine and the agent; the access stays yours.

Why do I bring my own AI account?

Mostly because you're already paying for ChatGPT or Claude, and I see no reason to charge you a markup on top of it. Your conversations stay inside an account you control, and if you ever cancel me, your AI subscription never notices. Your Wingman just runs on what you already have.

What can't it do?

It won't replace your judgment. Anything consequential ships as a draft and waits for your approval. It's also built for one person's work in one Slack; if you want something your whole team operates, that's a different conversation.

How fast is setup, really?

48 hours from checkout to live, counting from when you finish the intake and click your connect links. Your part takes about fifteen minutes. The rest of those two days is me building.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, no minimum. When the retainer ends I decommission the agent. Everything that matters lives in accounts you own, your AI subscription included, so there's nothing of yours for me to keep.

Why Slack?

Because you already live there, so there's no new app to remember to open. Your Wingman shows up as a teammate in the sidebar; you can DM it like a person or give it its own channel. Not on Slack? A free workspace takes five minutes and I'll set it up with you during onboarding.

Do I need to be technical?

No. That is the entire point of this offer. If you can send a Slack message and click an approve button, you can run a Wingman. The technical parts are my job, permanently.
