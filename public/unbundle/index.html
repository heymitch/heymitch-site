    See it in action.

    Input → Output

    "Charisma."

    ↓

    1. Make eye contact for one full beat before speaking.
    2. Speak about 15% slower than your default.
    3. Ask one question about their work before mentioning yours.
    4. Repeat one phrase they used, then build on it.
    5. Smile genuinely on the greeting and the goodbye.
    6. Stand or sit with weight evenly on both sides.
    7. Set a collaborative frame: "let's figure this out together."

    That's what "be more charismatic" actually means. Now imagine doing this for every piece of advice you've nodded at and never acted on.
